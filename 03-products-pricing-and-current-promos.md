- Sales page: [http://www.speakenglishwithvanessa.com/fluency](http://www.speakenglishwithvanessa.com/fluency)  
- New-student checkout (triggers $5 first month): [https://speak-english-with-vanessa.teachable.com/purchase?product\_id=3505735\&couponcode=30OFF](https://speak-english-with-vanessa.teachable.com/purchase?product_id=3505735&couponcode=30OFF)

- Edge case, not important for support: a $1 offer exists only on the free-PDF thank-you page and welcome sequence for students who haven't finished onboarding — for any support context, just use $5 and the /fluency page.  
- Includes all 248 lessons \+ private "\#1 English Community" group \+ daily email lessons.

### 2\. Yearly Membership ("FFC Yearly")

Not a default recommendation — only mention if specifically asked.

- Normally $420/year; promo pricing has been $230 (\~$190 off).  
- One-time-per-year payment, full access for the year.  
- PayPal cannot be used (recurring/subscription-style).  
- Monthly Membership students encounter the upgrade offer two ways: automatic upsell emails in their first month, and ongoing PS mentions in regular daily course emails — so a monthly student can realistically upgrade at any time, not just during promo windows.  
- Twice-a-year promotions run as two parallel campaigns: a public campaign (full pitch) and an existing-Monthly-member campaign (leads with "5 months free" vs. paying monthly all year).  
- Cancellation is different from Monthly: canceling does **not** cut off access immediately — access continues through the end of the paid year. Don't delete the subscription the way you would for Monthly; show them how to self-cancel via "Manage Subscriptions" in Teachable instead.  
- Refund: standard 30-day, no-hassle.

### 3\. Lifetime Membership ("FFC Lifetime")

"Lifetime" is current — "Unlimited" is an outdated name for the same plan (recognize it, don't use it in outgoing replies). Checkout: [http://www.speakenglishwithvanessa.com/lifetime-promo](http://www.speakenglishwithvanessa.com/lifetime-promo)

- Normally $1,000; promo pricing has been $450 (\~$550 off), sometimes with a 4-month plan ($125/mo × 4 \= $500).  
- One-time payment (or 4-pay plan), truly lifetime access, no recurring membership.  
- 90-day refund window (not the standard 30\) since it's the largest package.  
- Payment-plan risk: 3 failed installments auto-unenrolls the student — refund whatever they've already paid if they're upset about losing access.  
- Standing loyalty discount (always in effect, not promo-tied): $200+ total historical FFC spend → $650 off ($350 total); under $200 → $550 off ($450 total, also the standard promo price). Use total historical spend, not time-in-membership.  
- Zapier should auto-cancel the old Monthly Membership on upgrade, but sometimes fails — manually verify/cancel (Procedure A) if not caught.

## Speak With Vanessa AI

Separate product/app — unlimited AI conversation-practice partner with instant vocabulary/grammar/pronunciation feedback. Sales page: [https://speakenglishwithvanessa.com/speak-with-vanessa-ai/](https://speakenglishwithvanessa.com/speak-with-vanessa-ai/)

**What it is:** A web-based app (with a companion mobile app) where students get unlimited speaking practice with an AI clone of Vanessa, with instant feedback on vocabulary, grammar, and pronunciation. Students exchange voice recordings with the AI — each individual recording has a 1-minute limit, but there's no cap on how many recordings a student can send, so total practice time is unlimited even though each turn is capped at 1 minute. This is the direct answer to "how much can I talk with Vanessa AI." The app also tracks each student's passive and active vocabulary in a personalized dictionary, and students can click any word to see its meaning.

**Getting started:** Students can begin speaking with Vanessa AI immediately after signing up, no waiting period. Canceling is self-serve, anytime, via Profile → "Subscription Management" inside the app — this is the one thing to handle directly, send a reference screenshot if needed. Everything else ("I can't log in," app bugs, any question beyond canceling) — forward to Peter at [hi@yourteacher.ai](mailto:hi@yourteacher.ai) rather than troubleshooting internally.

- **Plans and standard (evergreen) pricing:** Monthly billing is the reference rate ($25/mo \= $300/year if paying month to month). Committing to the longer plans upfront gets a standing discount off that $300/year reference price:  
  - Monthly: $25/mo (no upfront commitment).  
  - 6-month: $107 total upfront ($17.83/mo effective) — a discount off the $300/year reference rate.  
  - 12-month (annual): $122 total upfront ($10.16/mo effective) — a deeper discount off the $300/year reference rate, since it's the longest commitment.  
  - This is why promo copy for SAVE70 (see file 08\) describes itself as "70% off the normal $300/year price" — $300/year is the standard monthly-equivalent reference price that all discounts (including the standing 6-/12-month rates above, and any time-limited promo) are measured against. It is not a separate/conflicting number from the $107/$122 rates — those are already-discounted prices relative to it.  
- **Trials:** 7 days (annual/6-month), 3 days (monthly).  
- **Coupon codes:**  
  1. **THANKS** — for students who completed the 30 Day Speaking Challenge (SC2): 40% off every month, ongoing.  
  2. **VANESSA** — for Fearless Fluency Club students only: first month free (Monthly plan), full price after. **Only share this code when the context confirms the person is an actual FFC student** — e.g. replying within an FFC course thread (Day 4/6, a daily-lesson reply, etc.), not to a general inquiry or a non-FFC lead. If it's unclear whether someone is an active FFC student, check Kit per file 04's membership-verification rule before sharing the code.  
  3. **SAVE70** — 70% off, time-limited, not a standing offer. Whether it's currently active is governed by file 08 (08-current-promo.md), not this file — check there first rather than assuming based on the note below. (Historically scheduled around September, but confirm against file 08/the promo calendar rather than assuming a date.)  
- **"30 Days, 30 Conversations" bonus challenge** (daily conversation-topic emails): a new topic emailed daily for 30 days, starting 2 days after signup (look for an email titled "Your challenge begins tomorrow"). Students can opt out of the daily emails if they want. **Not available to FFC students or current SC2 purchasers** — available to people who buy Vanessa AI outside those groups (e.g. via YouTube, or during a general-public promo like SAVE70). The one access exception is the temporary "No Promos: In FFC 3.0 Automation" tag applied during new FFC onboarding, which suppresses all marketing (not just this one) so new students stay focused — rare edge case, handle case-by-case, tag Vanessa if it comes up.  
- **Suggested marketing-style blurb**, if a student wants a fuller pitch rather than just facts: "Speaking with Vanessa AI helps you grow your speaking skills every day, any time. You'll have instant access and can start speaking with her today. The app tracks your passive and active vocabulary in a personalized dictionary, so you can see which words you know but aren't using yet when you speak — a great way to grow your vocabulary."

## Cross-product policies
