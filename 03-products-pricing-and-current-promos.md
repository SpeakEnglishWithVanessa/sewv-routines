## Core product: The Fearless Fluency Club (FFC)

As of July 1, 2024, separate one-time Level Courses (Beginner/Intermediate/Advanced) are no longer sold. Monthly Membership now includes all 248 FFC lessons. FFC is offered in three payment formats, all granting access to the same library:

### 1\. Monthly Membership ("FFC 3.0")

- Sales page: [http://www.speakenglishwithvanessa.com/fluency](http://www.speakenglishwithvanessa.com/fluency)  
- New-student checkout (triggers $5 first month): [https://speak-english-with-vanessa.teachable.com/purchase?product\_id=3505735\&couponcode=30OFF](https://speak-english-with-vanessa.teachable.com/purchase?product_id=3505735&couponcode=30OFF)  
- Full-price checkout (existing/rejoining — no coupon, $35): [https://speak-english-with-vanessa.teachable.com/purchase?product\_id=3505735](https://speak-english-with-vanessa.teachable.com/purchase?product_id=3505735)  
- couponcode=30OFF is the standing new-student rate, not tied to a rotating promo.  
- $35/month, first month $5 — the consistent standing rate, use this for support purposes.  
- Edge case, not important for support: a $1 offer exists only on the free-PDF thank-you page and welcome sequence for students who haven't finished onboarding — for any support context, just use $5 and the /fluency page.  
- Includes all 248 lessons \+ private "\#1 English Community" group \+ daily email lessons.  
- PayPal cannot be used (subscription payment) — see playbook \#16's PayPal exception.  
- Only format to recommend by default — don't volunteer Yearly/Lifetime/Challenges/Vanessa AI unless asked (playbook \#16).  
- Refund: standard 30-day, honored in practice even after. Confirm refunded/canceled and welcome to rejoin anytime — don't mention access timing. 

### 2\. Yearly Membership ("FFC Yearly")

Not a default recommendation — only mention if specifically asked.

- Normally $420/year; promo pricing has been $230 (\~$190 off).  
- One-time-per-year payment, full access for the year.  
- PayPal cannot be used (recurring/subscription-style).  
- Monthly Membership students encounter the upgrade offer two ways: automatic upsell emails in their first month, and ongoing PS mentions in regular daily course emails — so a monthly student can realistically upgrade at any time, not just during promo windows.  
- Twice-a-year promotions run as two parallel campaigns: a public campaign (full pitch) and an existing-Monthly-member campaign (leads with "5 months free" vs. paying monthly all year).  
- Cancellation is different from Monthly: canceling does **not** cut off access immediately — access continues through the end of the paid year. Don't delete the subscription the way you would for Monthly; show them how to self-cancel via "Manage Subscriptions" in Teachable instead.  
- Refund: standard 30-day, no-hassle.

### 3\. Lifetime Membership ("FFC Lifetime")

"Lifetime" is current — "Unlimited" is an outdated name for the same plan (recognize it, don't use it in outgoing replies). Checkout: [http://www.speakenglishwithvanessa.com/lifetime-promo](http://www.speakenglishwithvanessa.com/lifetime-promo)

- Normally $1,000; promo pricing has been $450 (\~$550 off), sometimes with a 4-month plan ($125/mo × 4 \= $500).  
- One-time payment (or 4-pay plan), truly lifetime access, no recurring membership.  
- 90-day refund window (not the standard 30\) since it's the largest package.  
- Payment-plan risk: 3 failed installments auto-unenrolls the student — refund whatever they've already paid if they're upset about losing access.  
- Standing loyalty discount (always in effect, not promo-tied): $200+ total historical FFC spend → $650 off ($350 total); under $200 → $550 off ($450 total, also the standard promo price). Use total historical spend, not time-in-membership.  
- Zapier should auto-cancel the old Monthly Membership on upgrade, but sometimes fails — manually verify/cancel (Procedure A) if not caught.

## Speak With Vanessa AI

Separate product/app — unlimited AI conversation-practice partner with instant vocabulary/grammar/pronunciation feedback. Sales page: [https://speakenglishwithvanessa.com/speak-with-vanessa-ai/](https://speakenglishwithvanessa.com/speak-with-vanessa-ai/)

- Plans: monthly ($25/mo), 6-month, or annual (\~$300/year, same effective rate as $25/mo). Coupon codes apply equally across all three tiers.  
- Trials: 7 days (annual/6-month), 3 days (monthly).  
- Three coupon codes:  
  1. **THANKS** — for students who completed the 30 Day Speaking Challenge (SC2): 40% off every month, ongoing.  
  2. **VANESSA** — for Fearless Fluency Club students only: first month free, full price after.  
  3. **SAVE70** — 70% off, but time-limited (scheduled for September, not a standing offer). Don't mention this outside its active window; check the promo calendar (file 04\) if unsure whether it's currently active.  
- "30 Days, 30 Conversations" bonus challenge (daily conversation-topic emails): not available to FFC students or current SC2 purchasers. Available to people who buy Vanessa AI outside a promo (e.g. via YouTube). Actively pushed during the SAVE70 window. The real access exception is the temporary "No Promos: In FFC 3.0 Automation" tag applied during new FFC onboarding, which suppresses all marketing (not just this one) so new students stay focused. Rare edge case — handle case-by-case, tag Vanessa if it comes up.  
- Cancellation (the one thing to handle directly): student self-serves via Profile → "Subscription Management" — send this instruction plus a reference screenshot if needed.  
- **Everything else** — "I can't log in," app bugs, any question beyond canceling — forward to Peter at [hi@yourteacher.ai](mailto:hi@yourteacher.ai) rather than troubleshooting internally.

## Challenges (standalone or bundled)

### 30 Day Listening Challenge

No longer sold individually — the main path in now is as an order bump on other checkout pages (Pack 5 most common, Pack 4 occasionally). No dedicated individual-pack checkout link.

- 150 Day Listening Challenge Bundle (all 5 packs) — only offered twice a year. Checkout: [http://www.speakenglishwithvanessa.com/150-lc-promo](http://www.speakenglishwithvanessa.com/150-lc-promo). Normally $500, promo $199 (60% off), or 4 monthly installments of $60.  
  - Already owns 1–2 packs: no extra stacking discount.  
  - Already owns 3 packs: sell the remaining 2 individually via a personal coupon (\~$60 each) rather than the bundle.  
  - Refund removal requires removing from each challenge's Kit sequence individually (no bulk option).

### 60 Day Word-Boosting Challenge (WBC)

Checkout: [http://www.speakenglishwithvanessa.com/60wb-promo](http://www.speakenglishwithvanessa.com/60wb-promo). Normally $200; promo $99 (one-time) or 3×$35. Vocabulary \+ phrasal-verb focused. Refund/removal: unenroll from Teachable, remove the Kit tag, and remove from the "WBC Daily Email" sequence specifically. Completion certificate available on request (Canva template, not official TESOL) — label Admin Archive/Certificate once sent.

### 90 Day English Challenge

Normally $300; promo $135 (one-time) or 3×$47. Combines PV \+ VC \+ LC4 ("Life"), running sequentially (\~30 days each). If someone already owns one component, refund/unenroll from the redundant one or manually enroll in a different pack (preference order: LC5, LC3, LC2, LC1). Removal requires going into the Kit automation directly for each of the 3 sequences (PV Daily Emails Promo, VC Daily Emails Promo, LC4 Daily Emails) — no bulk option.

### 30 Day Speaking Challenge ("SC2" internally, "30 Day Speaking Routine" to students)

Not evergreen — only runs during specific live windows, not open enrollment like FFC or the Listening Challenges. SC1 (original, 2022/23) is distinct from SC2 (current, launched July 2026, Vanessa-AI-integrated) — no theme name exists yet for SC1.

- Normally $70; promo $55.  
- Daily video lesson \+ daily speaking question via the Vanessa AI app (free, limited to 7 interactions/day without a full account, unlimited with one).  
- Standard refund policy, no exception.  
- Kit tag to opt out of just Speaking Challenge promos while staying on the newsletter: "Doesn't want SC promo."

## Cross-product policies

- **Testimonials:** if a student's message includes a usable testimonial (positive, specific, emotional, shows transformation), apply Admin Archive/Testimonial after replying. Comes up often in certificate-request replies.  
- **Refund default:** "30 days, no questions asked" is the stated policy; in practice the team honors requests well past 30 days for genuine cases. FFC Lifetime is 90 days by design.  
- **Promo rotation:** SEWV runs a different course/discount promo roughly monthly (tracked on Monday.com's "Important Links → Promotions" board, and the master Promotions calendar spreadsheet, see file 04). Each promo gets its own FAQ doc with current copy-paste answers — always check there first for anything price/promo-specific.
