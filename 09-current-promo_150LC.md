## PRODUCT OVERVIEW — What is the 150 Day Listening Challenge Bundle?

(Included here so general product questions can be answered alongside promo questions during this window. This is evergreen info about the bundle structure, not promo-specific — it should also live permanently in file 03 so it isn't lost once this promo file gets replaced.)

- **What it is:** A bundle of all five "30 Day Listening Challenge" packs (LC1–LC5), sold together as the "150 Day Listening Challenge." 5 months of daily listening lessons total, but access is lifetime — students can study at their own pace despite the "150 day" name.  
- **Content angle:** Helps students understand fast, natural English conversation. Features 50+ American English speakers across a range of accents, speeds, and vocabulary.  
- **Not evergreen, but comes up in support anyway:** This bundle is only actively sold twice a year (see file 03). Outside those windows there's no open checkout for it — but it still generates support email year-round because students sometimes purchase individual Listening Challenge packs as an upsell after finishing another course, then email in asking about the bundle, other packs, or this promo specifically. That's why the base product info lives in file 03 regardless of whether a bundle promo is currently running.  
- **Version check:** Make sure any link/reference is to the "150 Day Listening Challenge" (with challenges in brackets), NOT the outdated "150 Day English Listening Challenge" Teachable version — the word "English" in the title is the tell for the wrong/old version.  
- **Cohort structure:** Even though access is lifetime, this promo run is tied to a shared start date — see below. Everyone who buys during the sale window gets their Day 1 unlock email on the same day (the cohort start), not immediately on purchase.
