## PRODUCT OVERVIEW — What is Speak With Vanessa AI?

(Included here so general product questions can be answered alongside promo questions during this window. This is evergreen info, not promo-specific — it should also live permanently in file 03 so it isn't lost once this promo file gets replaced by the next one.)

- **What it is:** A web-based app (with a companion mobile app) where students get unlimited speaking practice with an AI clone of Vanessa. It gives instant feedback on vocabulary, grammar, and pronunciation as the student speaks.  
- **How "talking with Vanessa AI" actually works:** Students record voice messages back and forth with the AI. Each individual recording has a 1-minute limit, but there's no cap on how many recordings a student can send — so practice time itself is unlimited, just broken into 1-minute turns. This is the direct answer to "how much can I talk with Vanessa AI."  
- **Vocabulary tracking:** The app tracks each student's passive and active vocabulary in a personalized dictionary, and students can click any word to see its meaning — a way to see which words they recognize but aren't yet using when they speak.  
- **What to talk about:** Students can ask Vanessa AI anything English-related or just have a free-flowing conversation, like chatting with a friend. On top of that, new signups automatically get the **"30 Days, 30 Conversations"** bonus challenge — a new conversation topic emailed daily for 30 days, starting 2 days after signup (look for an email titled "Your challenge begins tomorrow"). Students can opt out of these daily emails if they want.  
- **Trial periods:** 7-day free trial on the annual or 6-month plans; 3-day free trial on the monthly plan.  
- **Plans:** Monthly, 6-month, or annual (yearly) — the yearly plan is what this SAVE70 promo applies to.  
- **Getting started:** Students can begin speaking with Vanessa AI immediately after signing up — no waiting period.  
- **Canceling:** Self-serve, anytime — Profile → "Subscription Management" inside the app.  
- **Support and bugs:** Anything beyond a general "what is this" question (login issues, app bugs, billing problems beyond this promo, refund requests) — forward to Peter at [hi@yourteacher.ai](mailto:hi@yourteacher.ai) rather than troubleshooting directly. This is standard practice regardless of the promo (see file 03).  
- **Suggested marketing-style blurb**, if a student wants a fuller pitch rather than just facts: "Speaking with Vanessa AI helps you grow your speaking skills every day, any time. You'll have instant access and can start speaking with her today. The app tracks your passive and active vocabulary in a personalized dictionary, so you can see which words you know but aren't using yet when you speak — a great way to grow your vocabulary."
