## Voice & phrasing patterns (still accurate — use these)

Beyond the standard format in 00-overview-and-workflow.md:

- Opening: "Thanks for your email. I'm so glad that you are learning English with me!"
- Empathetic opener for problems: "Thanks for your email, and I'm sorry about the problem/confusion."
- Closing: "I hope that helps. Keep up the good work with English!" (or the shorter "Keep up the good work with English!")
- "If you have any questions, feel free to ask" as a closing line, often paired with the above.
- Declining warmly: "I appreciate you thinking of me" / "I wish that things were different so you could join."
- Sensitive-topic sign-off: "I'll be thinking of you and hoping for peace and safety in your community. Take care." (Note: this sign-off pattern is for reference/tone only — per file 01 entry #19, financial-hardship/sanctions/restriction messages now always route to Claude/Need deleted with no reply at all, so this line won't actually be sent in that specific context anymore; it's kept here as a voice example for any other sensitive situation that does get a reply.)
- Refers to the course consistently as "The Fearless Fluency Club" (capitalized, with "The") in prose, not just "FFC."
- Time formatting: always "[time] EST (New York time zone)" — never just "EST" alone.
- "FILES" — whenever referencing the Facebook group's Files section, write it in all caps, bold, with quotation marks.
- No em dashes in email content — comma or colon instead. Internal editorial notes can still use dashes.
- Capitalize actual product names ("30 Day Listening Challenge," "The Fearless Fluency Club") but not generic words following them unless part of the official name.
