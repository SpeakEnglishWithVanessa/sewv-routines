## 5. Common student questions — how to answer

**"How do I win / what do I need to do to win?"**
New students: join FFC Monthly before September 5, and be on the Zoom call on September 5 at 9:00 AM EST when winners are drawn. Current members: just be on that same Zoom call — no purchase needed.

**"Do I have to join [FFC] to win?"** (from an already-enrolled student)
No — as a current member, attending the Zoom Celebration is what enters you. No additional purchase is needed.

**"What are the prizes?"**
One Golden Ticket (free lifetime FFC membership) and nine Silver Tickets (a free second month of FFC). Don't quote the per-Silver-Ticket dollar breakdown to a student — describe it as "a free second month," not a dollar figure.

**"Did I win?" / asking before or after the call**
Winners are announced live during the September 5 Zoom call — there's no separate notification sent to anyone who was eligible but not on the call. For any question about how winners are picked, whether a student needs to prepare anything, or whether they've won: the standard reply is simply that the winner will be chosen live on the call, and there's nothing special they need to do to prepare — just be on the call. Use this same answer even for win-dispute-style questions ("I think I should have won," "how do I know if I won") — no account lookup needed, no routing to admin for this.

**"What if I can't make the Zoom call — can I still win / is there a recording or makeup?"**
No exception exists for winning: attendance on the live call is how winners are entered/drawn, for both new and current members — this doesn't change even if a student watches the recording afterward. There will be a recording of the celebration available for students to watch, and it's fine to mention that as something they can still enjoy, but be clear that only students who were live on the call are eligible to win. Don't promise any alternate entry or makeup drawing beyond watching the recording — if a student pushes further than that, route to Claude/Need admin or flag Vanessa rather than improvising an accommodation.

**"Is the $5 price only good until the promo ends?"**
No — clarify this is FFC Monthly's standard ongoing trial rate, not a limited-time sale price. It won't change after September 5.

**Timezone/"I can't join at 9 AM EST" questions**
Handle like any other Sunday-lesson timezone question (file 06) — acknowledge the difficulty, no separate call time is offered for this one-time event.
